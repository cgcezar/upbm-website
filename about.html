Who we are

A campus-based mountaineering organization

The University of the Philippines Baguio Mountaineers (UPBM) is a non-profit, campus-based organization that promotes mountaineering as both a venue for environmental consciousness and a healthy, sustainable physical activity.

In 2003, UPBM pioneered the first trail-running event in Baguio City, advocating for safer open spaces and accessible green environments. The call for the proper preservation and maintenance of nature trails and parks was well received, and the Run for Trails has since become a patronized annual event in the city.

Beyond climbs, the organization runs training programs, environmental advocacy drives, and community initiatives — carrying the belief that mountaineering is not just about reaching the summit, but going beyond it.
