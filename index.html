Our purpose

"Mountaineering is not just about literally reaching the summit, but going beyond it."

The UP Baguio Mountaineers pioneered the first trail-running event in Baguio City in 2003, calling for the preservation and maintenance of nature trails and open green spaces. That advocacy lives on today through climbs, training, and community programs.
